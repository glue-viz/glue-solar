"Browse observations…" in the Plugins menu lists a folder of IRIS Level 2 files and SST cubes by observation, searched in the background, with a time range, a text filter, saved folders and recent searches. Tick raster windows, slit-jaw channels, AIA cutouts, Hinode/SOT cubes, SST cubes or packed downloads to load or unpack them; "Stack sequential raster scans" loads each window's scans as one cube, and "Open quicklook" opens the result in a quicklook.
