DKIST Level 1 datasets open from their ASDF file, VBI and DL-NIRSP mosaics as one dataset per tile, and save in sessions. "Quicklook…" opens ViSP and Cryo-NIRSP rasters, with their context imager if loaded; glue offers to link DKIST datasets to each other, and "Link helioprojective coordinates" links them to IRIS data. "Mosaic…", "Frame quality…", "Download these frames…" and "DKIST footprints…" stitch tiles, plot seeing, fetch frames through Globus and draw footprints.
