Analyses through irispy are in a dataset's right-click menu. "IRIS: line maps…" (line moments, Doppler image, red-blue asymmetry), "IRIS: Mg II features…", "IRIS: detect UV bursts…" and "IRIS: subtract mean spectrum" run in the background on IRIS raster windows and stacks of scans; line maps, Mg II features and the mean spectrum leave out or flag saturated samples. "Line ratio diagnostic…" maps a density or temperature from two line maps, with an O IV density preset.
