A quicklook now takes its observation's aligned AIA cutouts and Hinode/SOT cubes as it takes slit-jaw images: each is in the Panels list under "AIA" or "SOT", unticked, and once ticked is a panel that follows the time, marks the point, draws the raster overlays and moves the point at a click. These cubes link with the observation's rasters and slit-jaw images as it loads; one of another observation links on request, with "Link helioprojective coordinates".
