Profile viewers mark the main lines of IRIS, or of ground-based telescopes on DKIST and SST data, with "Main lines", list the lines in a range with "Lines in this range…", and show the Doppler velocity from a rest wavelength with "Velocity axis". The "Gaussian + constant" fitter builds models of Gaussian and Lorentzian lines on a background, with bounds and ties, and "Fit every spectrum" fits every spectrum of an IRIS raster window or stack with it in parallel, giving velocity, width and intensity maps.
