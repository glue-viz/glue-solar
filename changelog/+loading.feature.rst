IRIS Level 2 raster files, slit-jaw images, aligned AIA cutouts and Hinode/SOT cubes open through irispy, from the observation browser or "File → Open Data Set", and 16-bit data are read from their files as they are viewed. Each raster window, slit-jaw image and AIA cutout has ``Time``, ``Exposure time``, ``<window> DN / s`` and ``<window> mask`` attributes; wavelengths are in Angstrom and helioprojective coordinates in arcsec, missing samples are NaN, saturated IRIS samples are marked in the mask, and a stack keeps each scan's own coordinates.
