The quicklook is faster: a click, a slit step and a synced step now meet the M0 time budgets on full observations. Image viewers draw fewer times, glue's axis labels no longer place every tick at each axis reset, and repeated coordinate conversions are reused. A new Hide axes button, with a ``SOLAR_SHOW_AXES`` setting for new viewers, hides an Image viewer's axes, which roughly halves its draw time.
