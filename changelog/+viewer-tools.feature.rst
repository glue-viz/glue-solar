Image viewers gain a View menu ("Frame time", "Hide axes", "Per-frame limits", "Wavelength band…", "Physical aspect", "Color bar", "Raster overlays", "IRIS field of view", "DKIST footprints…", "Cursor readout"), a Coordinate menu ("Lead the time", "Go to UTC…", "Loop…" and the "Point" and "Blink" submenus), "Follow/lock", "Measure" and four Gamma stretches. The keys D and F step in time, A and S in wavelength, and Space plays.
