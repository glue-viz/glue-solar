IRIS raster windows, stacks of scans, slit-jaw images and AIA cutouts stored as 16-bit integers now load lazily: they keep their raw values on disk and scale only what a viewer shows, so every window of a large raster opens in a fraction of a second. The mask is a derived ``<window> mask`` component, and ``glue_solar.sources.loaders.iris.LAZY = False`` restores the previous in-memory loading.
