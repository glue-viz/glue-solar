IRIS raster windows, stacks of scans, slit-jaw images and AIA cutouts stored as 16-bit integers now load lazily: they keep their raw values on disk and scale only what a viewer shows, so opening every window of a 7 GB raster takes a fraction of a second and a few megabytes of memory. Colour limits are exact percentiles of every value, the mask is a derived ``<label> mask`` component, and ``glue_solar.sources.loaders.iris.LAZY = False`` restores the previous in-memory loading.
