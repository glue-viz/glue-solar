IRIS rasters, slit-jaw images and stacked rasters load the missing-data values -200 and -199 as NaN, so default image limits and Mean profiles no longer include them and the gaps draw transparently. AIA cutouts treat only -200 as missing and stay integer when they have none. Negative-step rasters get NaN at the right steps, because the missing samples are found in the flipped data rather than in irispy's unflipped mask.
