The observation browser ("Browse observations…" in the Plugins menu) lists a folder of IRIS Level 2 files, its subfolders included, by observation: one row per OBSID and start time with description, pointing and file counts. Tick slit-jaw bands, raster windows or aligned AIA cutouts to load them, or a packed ``*.tar.gz`` download to unpack it in place. Rasters keep each exposure's time, and helioprojective coordinates show in arcsec. It replaces the one-directory importer and the ``qglue`` parsers.
