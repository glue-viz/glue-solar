"Quicklook…" in a dataset's right-click menu, the browser's "Open quicklook" or ``glue --startup=iris_quicklook`` opens an IRIS observation as linked panels in a new tab, in the manner of CRISPEX: the raster map, spectrogram and wavelength panel, each slit-jaw image and the spectrum at a point. One point and one time drive every panel. A Point window lists the point's values, a Panels list turns panels, other raster windows and AIA and SOT cubes on and off, a region drawn on the map or a slit-jaw image selects the same place in the other data, and light curves, rows and columns follow the point.
