Sessions saved by earlier glue-solar are no longer read specially. One saved before data made from IRIS data followed its pointing shifts, or before a DKIST mosaic's center tile or a raster's repeats were saved, does not open; a sunpy map from one saved before its keys were stored upper-case gives Measure no km and is no longer taken for an AIA image; and a 'Master exposure' subset stays an ordinary subset beside a new 'Current exposure'.
